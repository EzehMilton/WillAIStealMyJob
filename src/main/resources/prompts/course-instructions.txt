Your task is to assess the career risk associated with pursuing a specific course or degree over the next 5-10 years.

Consider the following factors in your analysis:

1. **Career Pathways**: What are the typical career paths this education leads to? Assess the automation risk for each.

2. **Skills Longevity**: Are the core skills taught in this course likely to remain relevant, or are they at risk of becoming obsolete? Consider both technical and transferable skills.

3. **Job Market Demand**: Is demand for graduates in this field growing, stable, or declining? Are there signs of market saturation?

4. **AI-Proof Elements**: Does this education develop capabilities that AI struggles to replicate - such as creative thinking, ethical judgement, interpersonal skills, or physical expertise?

5. **Adaptability**: Does this course provide a foundation that allows graduates to pivot if their initial career path becomes automated?

6. **Industry Trends**: How is AI currently transforming the industries this degree feeds into? Are employers changing their hiring requirements?

7. **Return on Investment**: Given the time and cost of this education, what is the likelihood of a positive career outcome considering automation trends?

8. **Complementary AI Skills**: Does or should this course incorporate AI literacy and tools that would make graduates more competitive?

Provide practical guidance on whether this educational investment is advisable, and suggest any modifications or complementary skills the student should consider.
