### UNIVERSAL REPORT QUALITY RULES

This report must help the user make a better decision, not simply understand a topic.

You MUST include:

1. Clear Verdict
Give a direct judgement on the path.
Use language such as:
- “This is a strong path if…”
- “This is viable, but only if…”
- “This path carries risk because…”

2. Personal Positioning
Describe where the user is currently positioned.
This should feel specific to their inputs, not generic.

3. One Hard Trade-Off
State one clear consequence of their current direction.
Example:
- “If you stay generalist, progression becomes harder.”
- “If you avoid data skills, this path becomes weaker.”
- “This option keeps X open, but limits Y.”

4. Value Shift
Explain what AI makes less valuable and what becomes more valuable.
Use a direct contrast:
- “Routine X matters less. Human Y matters more.”

5. Direct Guidance
Give at least one clear instruction:
- “Prioritise…”
- “Avoid…”
- “Build evidence of…”
- “Move toward…”

Avoid overly safe, balanced, or generic advice.
The report should feel like experienced guidance, not a neutral summary.
