WillAIStealMyFuture · How it works

A clear, personalised view of AI risk without the guesswork.

The assessment turns your role, course, or subject direction into a structured report that explains where AI is most likely to create pressure, where human strengths still matter, and what you can do next.

01 · Context

We start with your situation

You choose the path that fits you: current profession, university course, or school subject choices. The details you provide help the system avoid generic career advice.

02 · Analysis

We assess exposure at the practical level

The report looks beyond job titles. It considers the kinds of tasks, decisions, skills, career routes, and study choices that are more or less exposed to AI-driven change.

03 · Action

We turn the findings into a plan

You receive a readable summary first. The full report expands that into a more detailed outlook, practical priorities, and steps you can act on without needing to be an AI expert.

What the report is designed to do

The aim is not to predict your future with false certainty. The aim is to help you make better decisions while AI is changing how work, study, hiring, and skills are valued.

  • Separate broad AI hype from the parts of your path that actually deserve attention.
  • Show likely pressure points without presenting them as fixed destiny.
  • Highlight skills and directions that improve resilience, adaptability, and optionality.
  • Give you a practical plan that is specific enough to use, but simple enough to start.

The exact scoring methods, weighting, prompt design, validation checks, and report assembly process are proprietary. We share the reasoning at a useful level without exposing the internal system that produces the analysis.
